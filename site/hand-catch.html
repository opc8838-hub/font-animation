接住 Hand Catch

手机斜着落下，右手接住并转正。停顿后视角绕转，随后从斜侧角度重播接住。

A phone falls at an angle. A right hand catches it and brings it upright. After a pause, the view orbits, then cuts to a replay from the side.

制作流程 Making of

画得像 Keyframes

用自制 3D 渲染起一张手部定妆图。以对应时刻的画面为编辑对象，以定妆图为身份参考，用 Codex 生图编辑 22 张关键帧。只重画手，保留构图、手机姿态和运动模糊。

Build the hand identity image from an in-house 3D render. Use it as the identity reference while editing 22 keyframes in Codex. Redraw the hand and preserve framing, phone pose and motion blur.

动起来 First-to-last-frame generation

把相邻关键帧交给海螺 MiniMax-H3-Max，每段生成 5 秒、768P，共 18 段。提示词分别描述下落、接住、镜头绕转和停住。

Use adjacent keyframes as endpoints in MiniMax-H3-Max to generate 18 five-second clips at 768P. Write separate prompts for falling, catching, camera orbit and holding.

顺不顺 Code-based edit

用光流量出每段的实际运动进度，再按实测速度重排时间。关键帧接口在 ±0.06 秒内做光流变形，加入 180° 快门运动模糊。「接住又松手」的一段用相似帧剪接修复，整片保留 11.31 秒的一次硬切。

Measure each clip’s motion with optical flow and retime it to the measured reference speed. Warp across joins within ±0.06 seconds and apply 180° shutter blur. Match frames to repair a catch-and-release clip, leaving the hard cut at 11.31 seconds.
